AyuGram Sync Preview 0.1

Take your saved deleted messages to a new computer

Telegram loads your current chats on the new PC. The deleted messages and earlier versions AyuGram saved stay on the old one. AyuGram Sync moves them into a separate archive.

Download ZIP Quick start

Manual transfer of private chat text.

Try it without your own chats

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open a terminal in the extracted folder. You need Python 3.9+.
  3. Run the command for your system. The test transfers a sample edit and deletion and prints OK.

Transfer your own chat

Quit AyuGram Desktop, copy its database and choose one private chat. Import its records into your archive and open them on a second PC. This preview transfers text from Desktop manually.
