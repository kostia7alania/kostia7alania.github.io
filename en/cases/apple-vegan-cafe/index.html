Multilingual website

Apple Vegan Cafe

Menu, opening hours and contacts for a family-run cafe in Pattaya, in English, Thai and Russian.

My role
Website engineering and content model
Technology
Astro, Sveltia CMS, Playwright
Evidence
Live website

How it works

  1. Choose a language
  2. Browse menu and hours
  3. Find contact and ordering options

The problem

Guests often find the cafe and open the menu on their phones. They need current dishes, the address and opening hours in a familiar language. The family needs a way to update that information without editing code.

My contribution

I built a static Astro site and a shared dataset for all three languages. The core menu works without JavaScript, with a small script adding filtering. I set up a Git-backed content editor and added browser checks for guest journeys.

The outcome

Guests can browse the menu, check hours, contact the cafe or go to ordering. The website is live on its own domain and the source is on GitHub.
