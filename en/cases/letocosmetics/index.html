Storefront modernization

Letocosmetics

An Astro and Payload storefront with a catalog and product pages in a separate staging environment. The live store runs on Bitrix.

My role
Storefront and migration preparation
Technology
Astro, Payload, PostgreSQL, Bitrix
Evidence
Storefront on staging

How it works

  1. Compare with Bitrix
  2. Build the storefront
  3. Verify on staging

The problem

The store already has a catalog, product pages and working links. Changes to the storefront need a separate environment where the existing customer journeys can be compared with the new version.

My contribution

I worked on a separate Astro storefront with a catalog, search and product pages. Its staging content uses Payload and PostgreSQL. I compared routes with the live store and checked catalog, search and product navigation on mobile.

The outcome

The catalog and product pages can be reviewed on staging before a store migration decision. The link below opens the existing Bitrix storefront.
